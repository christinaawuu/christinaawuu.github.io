Hi, I'm Christina! I'm a New Yorker from Brooklyn, a game developer and software engineer, and a 2025 computer science graduate of USC.

I like the part of games where code meets people. I build the gameplay systems, then watch real players try them out and figure out what makes them smile or get stuck. I've made games in Unity and Unreal, run 50+ playtest sessions, and worked as both an engineer and a producer, so I'm comfortable in the conversation between design and code.

I also build iOS apps and websites, make 3D models and animations in Maya, and have a marketing and community side from my time with USC Own It and Women in Engineering.

When I'm not at a keyboard, you'll find me:

- biking along the West Side Highway
- wandering around DUMBO
- having picnics in Prospect Park and Central Park
- working out at the gym
- eating my way through the city, because I'm a total foodie

Wander around the map to see my projects, my experience, and a few of my favorite spots. If something catches your eye, I'd love to chat.
